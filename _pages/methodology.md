    <h1 class="typography-info-headline">How Harvee reviews guide content</h1>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">Last reviewed: August 26, 2026</p>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">Harvee’s guides are designed to help people understand Apple Watch-based wellness tools, not to diagnose conditions or replace clinical care. We publish a guide only after its claims, comparisons, and links have been reviewed for relevance to the page’s specific question.</p>

    <h2 class="medium-8">What we check</h2>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">For device and software guides, we check the applicable Apple Watch model, operating-system compatibility, and the limits of the workflow described. For comparison guides, we distinguish between a product’s stated features, its documented hardware or subscription requirements, and Harvee’s own capabilities. A comparison should also identify situations in which the other product may be a better fit.</p>

    <h2 class="medium-8">Sources and dates</h2>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">Each approved guide links to the sources used for its factual claims. These may include first-party product documentation, official support material, peer-reviewed research, and other directly relevant primary sources. The page’s “Last reviewed” date identifies the editorial and factual review; its sitemap date identifies the last material update. We remove a guide from search indexing when its evidence, differentiation, or freshness no longer meets this standard.</p>

    <h2 class="medium-8">What our comparisons do not claim</h2>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">Harvee does not present its guides as clinical validation, diagnostic advice, or a guarantee that one wearable will be more accurate for every person. Wearable readings can vary with device configuration, wear time, context, and the underlying measurement method. Readers should consult a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment decisions.</p>

    <h2 class="medium-8">Commercial transparency</h2>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">Harvee may describe its own product and invite readers to download it. That commercial relationship does not remove the need to state limitations, name relevant alternatives, or link to the evidence behind material factual claims. Time-sensitive ratings, rankings, awards, pricing, and feature claims are shown only when a public source and review date are recorded.</p>

    <h2 class="medium-8">Request a correction</h2>
    <p class="large-12 medium-12">If you find an inaccurate, outdated, or unclear claim, please contact <a href="mailto:feedback@harvee.app">feedback@harvee.app</a> with the guide URL and supporting source. We will review substantiated corrections as part of the next editorial update.</p>
