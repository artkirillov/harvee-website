## First-gen Ultra: still the right hardware for HRV trends

Apple Watch Ultra — the original — remains one of the most capable wrists Apple has shipped. Titanium case, action button, dual-frequency GPS, multi-day battery. Owners who don't upgrade every cycle still have a sensor array that records HRV overnight, tracks sleep, and logs resting heart rate into HealthKit every day they wear it.

Capability without interpretation is incomplete. Ultra celebrates workouts. Health stores numbers. Neither answers whether twelve days of declining overnight HRV preceded the week you felt bulletproof until you weren't. Harvee reads that slope.

### Why Ultra owners benefit disproportionately

Ultra buyers tend to push — physically and professionally. Long events, early flights, back-to-back training. The culture rewards output. Recovery signals are easy to ignore when the watch still shows green rings and the battery lasts through a two-day race.

Ultra's battery advantage matters for HRV continuity. Series owners who charge nightly sometimes skip sleep wear. Ultra owners often don't. More complete nights mean cleaner baselines and earlier detection of sustained suppression — the pattern that precedes overtraining and burnout.

### Ultra HRV vs workout metrics

Strava celebrates the effort. Harvee asks whether your autonomic nervous system kept up. Both questions matter. If you only track the first, you'll periodically discover the second the hard way — illness, injury, or emotional flatness after a heroic month.

Harvee doesn't replace sport-specific tools. It complements them with recovery framing from the same Apple Watch already on your wrist.

### No upgrade required for interpretation

Ultra 2 adds refinements, but HealthKit HRV from original Ultra is fully supported. If you're considering Ultra 2 for GPS or display upgrades, do that. If you're considering it for Harvee — don't. Connect Harvee to Health, wear Ultra to sleep, let week two tell you what week one couldn't.

Harvee is wellness insight, not medical diagnosis. Bring concerning trends to qualified professionals when needed.
