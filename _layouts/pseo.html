Try it free

Try it. You'll know within a week.

{% if page.risk_reversal_body %} {{ page.risk_reversal_body | markdownify }} {% else %}

Harvee is free to download. The Today tab and Playbook — your daily stress view and the education section — are fully available without a subscription. You can see how Harvee reads your Apple Watch data within the first few days.

The Patterns tab, where long-term HRV trends and stress charts live, shows demo data until you subscribe to Harvee+. Even without subscribing, a week of daily use on the Today tab gives you a clear sense of whether the insights match how your body actually feels.

Harvee compares your readings against rolling 7-day and 4-week baselines — so the first week establishes context, and the second week starts to show direction. If it's not useful, delete the app. Subscriptions and refunds are handled entirely through Apple; you can request a refund via Apple Support if needed.

Why we built it

Why Harvee exists

{% if page.uniqueness_body %} {{ page.uniqueness_body | markdownify }} {% else %}

Most health apps answer one question: what is my number right now? Harvee was built around a different question: what has my nervous system been doing, and what does that mean for the week I'm in?

Your Apple Watch collects HRV passively — during sleep, between meetings, on rest days and hard ones. That data only becomes useful when something connects the dots across days, weights them against your personal baseline, and shows you the pattern before you hit the wall.

Harvee is an Apple Watch stress app built for pattern recognition over time, not daily scores in isolation. Apple recognised it as a Design Awards Finalist for Social Impact. Not for how it looks. For what it does to people's lives.
